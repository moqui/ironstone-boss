# IronStone BOSS — Core-app design review

Oct 28, 2026 — review findings, before fixes

This reviews the Oct 27 drafts of [design-crosscut.md](design-crosscut.md) and the six core-app designs: [Accounting](design-accounting.md), [Customer Service](design-customer-service.md), [Manufacturing](design-manufacturing.md), [Products](design-products.md), [Purchasing](design-purchasing.md) and [Warehouse](design-warehouse.md). Each design was checked against its `research-*.md`, the [design-doc-guide.md](design-doc-guide.md) checklist, active Mantle XML, the built BOSS screens, and the reference apps in `~/work/moquiwork/3p_other`. The crosscut file's own §6.3 review covered structure and names; this one covers substance: research coverage, existing behaviour the designs build on, the handoffs between apps, and features the reference apps have.

**How it was done.** A script first checked names, links, needs IDs, the registry against the app mounts, and the inventories (§8). One reviewer per file then went through research coverage, the checklist, internal consistency, its side of each interface, reference features and the Open list. Two more reviewers walked the end-to-end flows across the files arrow by arrow, tested each cross-app finding and chose an owner wherever a step had none or two. The blockers were then re-checked against Mantle and the BOSS code by hand.

**How to read it.**
- §2 is the patterns behind most findings.
- §3 is the cross-app decisions, each with a recommendation.
- §4 is the steps that need one owner.
- §5 is every finding by file.
- §6 is the reference features, sized.
- §7 is the verdicts on each file's Open list.

**What happens next.** The fix pass applies the §3 recommendations and every finding in §5 that you don't strike. It adds the S-sized reference features in §6 to the designs. M and L features go into each file's Open list with a recommendation, unless you promote them. One editor per file applies the fixes, then the crosscut roll-up and the two inventories are updated, and the checks in §8 are run again. Placeholder screen files are not renamed, because that is code.

## 2. What the findings have in common

Research coverage is good. Every research open question in all six files is answered in Decided, though two Customer Service answers rest on a model the design doesn't define. Of about 235 candidate screen entries, one is missing (Customer Service's suggest-an-edit), and about 15 are unclear or only named. Most findings are about depth instead: what existing code already does, and what the other app on a handoff must do.

1. **Existing Mantle automation the designs never mention.** Most of the blockers come from here. These SECAs and services run today, and every design that touches them must say whether each is kept, conditioned or replaced:
   - **Supplier bill on receipt.** `ShipmentIncomingDeliveredCreateInvoices` creates a supplier bill when a receipt is delivered, and it bills rejected and quarantined units at the PO price (R-XC-01, R-SB-01).
   - **Sales invoice at pack.** `ShipmentOutgoingPackedCreateInvoices` creates the sales invoice and captures card payment at pack. After that, nothing can be unpacked (R-WH-01, R-SB-17).
   - **Return approval.** `ReturnApprovedProcess` issues immediate refunds and replacements, with no finance capability needed (R-SB-02).
   - **Return receipt.** `ShipmentDeliveredReturnReceive` → `ReturnReceivedProcess` settles before inspection (R-WH-01, R-SB-03).
   - **Order auto-approval.** `OrderPlacedAutoApprove` approves POs without review, and sales orders without a credit check (R-SB-09, R-SB-10).
   - **Returning unused material.** `revert#AssetIssuanceOnCancel` unposts and deletes GL entries instead of reversing them (R-SA-02).
   - **Production run materials.** `create#ProductionRun` writes material lines from the legacy single-level `PatMfgBom` (R-MFG-01).
   - **Request to PO.** `create#PurchaseRequestOrder` re-sources every request item on each call (R-PUR-01).
2. **Existing names used as the wrong basis.** The services and fields below don't do what the designs assume:
   - `send#OrderStoreEmail` sends nothing without a store (R-PUR-02).
   - `cancel#OrderItem` measures outbound issuance, not receipt (R-PUR-03).
   - `modify#ProductPrice` is a resolver hook, not a store service (R-PUR-04, R-PRD-05).
   - `ShipmentItemSource.quantityNotHandled` is the quantity still to handle, not a shortfall (R-WH-02).
   - `ReturnHeader.externalId` is an integration key, not the customer's reference (R-CS-06).
   - `SettlementTerm` has no discount or installment fields (R-ACC-12).
   - `AcctgTransEntry` has no party or facility dimension (R-ACC-11).
   - Foreign-currency documents are posted with no conversion to the base currency (R-ACC-01).
   - `receive#EntireShipment` accepts units that never arrived (R-SB-11).
3. **Contracts with only one side.** In each of these, one app relies on a service that the owning app never defines:
   - Budget commitments (R-ACC-06, R-PUR-07)
   - Credit check and hold (R-ACC-05, R-SB-09)
   - Bill hold and its enforcement on every payment path (R-ACC-04, R-SB-16)
   - Production cost events (R-MFG-02, R-XC-08)
   - Approval topics and their handlers (R-PUR-14)
   - Product lifecycle enforcement (R-PRD-04)
   - Pack-scan resolution (R-PRD-03)
   - Match policy (R-PUR-08)
   - Service-line acceptance (R-PUR-09)
   - Stock at date (R-WH-06)
   - The RMA return label (R-CS-08)
4. **The cost and subcontract flows have no owner end to end.** No file says how production cost reaches the GL alongside Mantle's existing issue and receipt postings (R-SA-01). No file says who sets the run's owner, which otherwise books every issue to COGS (R-SA-03), or who closes the run (R-SA-11). Nobody owns dispatching, consuming or receiving subcontract material (R-XC-07, R-SA-08, R-SA-13). The same goes for unpack and repack (R-WH-07), the baseline bulk cost correction (R-ACC-02, R-PRD-14) and landed cost (R-ACC-10, R-PUR-18).
5. **New stores that duplicate built ones.** Use what is already there:
   - Saved replies: Comms `EmailTextBlock` (R-CS-01)
   - Recurring invoices: Mantle recurring orders (R-ACC-13)
   - Downtime maintenance: `AssetMaintenance` (R-MFG-06)
   - Portal links: the MoquiConf token routes, with one shared capability service instead of one per app (R-XC-03, R-SB-14)
   - Invoice state changes: the Projects `BillingServices` guard, to become a single guard (R-XC-04)
6. **Security boundaries.**
   - Customer and supplier portal pages sit under internal app roots, which need the app grant and run org-setup actions (R-ACC-03, R-PUR-12).
   - The Products Units screen can edit exchange rates (R-PRD-01).
   - Return approval settles money without a finance capability (R-SB-02).
   - The capability names used across apps map to no user group (R-ACC-15, R-CS-07).
   - Case rules apply only on Customer Service screens, while five other entry points create and close the same Requests (R-CS-04, R-CS-05).
7. **The shared-screen registry is incomplete.**
   - Several mounts have no per-app context row; Manufacturing is missing from five shared screens. RequestDetail, ContactDetail and Approvals have no per-app context table at all (R-XC-11 to R-XC-14).
   - The crosscut §9 Open list is empty, although nine app Open items are cross-app (R-XC-15).
   - Two needs folds drop named work (R-XC-17).
8. **Reference features most often missing.** Each is sized in §6:
   - Landed costs, cost centres or ledger dimensions, early-payment discounts, bank-to-bank transfers, cash-basis and withholding tax.
   - Scrap and disposal, cycle-count schedules, lot and serial generation on receipt, rate shipping without a gateway.
   - Kit definitions, separate sellable and purchasable flags, a Connections pane on Product360.
   - Drop-ship, min/max reorder levels, unbuild, component reservation and staging.
   - Warranty terms with a serial-to-sale lookup.

## 8. Mechanical checks

A script checked every design file before the reviewers read it. The results are mechanical; the reviewers confirmed or dismissed each.

- **Links and anchors.** Every relative link and `#anchor` in the seven files resolves, including the `../../mr-pass/plan/` links.
- **Mantle names.** Every fully qualified service, entity, field and status/enum name was checked against active XML (comments stripped) in mr-mantle, mr-boss, mr-marble and the framework. The names that do not exist are proposed and labelled so. The exceptions are names that exist but are the wrong basis, which are findings in §5 (for example `send#OrderStoreEmail`, `cancel#OrderItem`, `modify#ProductPrice`, `ShipmentItemSource.quantityNotHandled`, `ReturnHeader.externalId`, `SettlementTerm`, `AcctgTransEntry` dimensions).
- **Screen names.**
  - Abbreviations that break design-doc-guide §10: `Boms`, `BomDetail`, `BomExplosion`, `BomCompare` and `BomVarianceReport` (Manufacturing and the crosscut registry); `Rfqs`, `RfqDetail` and `RfqResponse` (Purchasing); `GlAccountDetail` (Accounting).
  - The acronyms `SlaCompliance`, `SlaPolicies` and `CsatResults` (Customer Service) are industry terms. Keep them, and add them to §10 as allowed.
  - Screen names that differ from their path segment without the repeat that §4 allows: Manufacturing `MaterialIssueReceipt` (…/Materials), `ProductionTime` (…/Time), `QualityGates` (…/Quality), `ProductionCosts` (…/Costs), `ResourceCalendar` (…/Calendar), `SubcontractOperations` (…/Subcontracting), `ProductionSettings` (…/Settings); Products `InventoryAvailability` (…/Stock) and `CategoryContent` (…/Content).
  - Warehouse `Count` at `Warehouse/Handheld/Count` breaks the `Handheld*` pattern of its siblings.
- **Archetypes.** All values are valid. style-guide §3.15 **Tree list** is missing from design-doc-guide §4's list.
- **Registry and mounts.** These app mounts have no per-app context row in crosscut §3:
  - Manufacturing: Supplier360, OrderDetail, ShipmentDetail, RequestDetail, WorkItemDetail, Calendar, Chatter/RecordFiles and Approvals.
  - Customer Service: ContactDetail, CaseDetail and CustomerCase (both are RequestDetail mounts).
  - Purchasing and Warehouse: RequestDetail.
  - Accounting and Purchasing: Approvals.

  §3.11 RequestDetail has form tables but no per-app context table. §3.1 and §3.12–§3.15 have none.
