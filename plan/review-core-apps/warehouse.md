# Review: design-warehouse.md (Oct 27, 2026 draft)

Reviewed Oct 28, 2026 against research-warehouse.md, design-crosscut.md, design-doc-guide.md §11, style-guide §3/§7/§8.2, design-projects §6, design-purchasing/manufacturing/customer-service/accounting/products, the inventories, active Mantle XML (with comments removed) and the reference checkouts. Line numbers are `design-warehouse.md:N` unless another file is named. Mantle paths are relative to `runtime/component/mr-mantle/`. Third-party paths are relative to `~/work/moquiwork/3p_other/`.

Pre-check disposition: the Handheld* names that differ from their path segments are dismissed, because guide §4 allows this when the segment alone would repeat (Receive, Pick, Pack, Move, Putaway, Home). `Count` is a nit (R-WH-31). The RequestDetail registry gap is dismissed: the Warehouse row is at design-projects.md:1287, not in crosscut §3.11. The differences against that row are R-WH-25. The ASSERTED name flags are dismissed. `CarrierServices.get#ShipmentRates` (line 29) is explicitly called commented out, and it is commented in CarrierServices.xml:565. The QualityServices and InspectionTemplate names are crosscut §3.19 proposals. Of the 16 blank Asset inventory forms, the maintenance, meter, registration and custodian forms are not Warehouse's. FindAsset `UpdateProductCost`, `CreateEquipment` and AssetDetail `UpdateAssetAcquireCost` need a Decided row (R-WH-23). The "Locations" menu word flag and the placeholder check are dismissed.
