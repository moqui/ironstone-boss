# Review: design-purchasing.md (Oct 27, 2026 draft)

Reviewed Oct 28, 2026 against research-purchasing.md, design-crosscut.md, design-doc-guide.md §11, the
owning designs (Products, Warehouse, Manufacturing, Accounting, Projects, Commons, Customer Service),
comment-stripped active XML in `runtime/component/mr-mantle` and `runtime/base-component`, and the
reference checkouts under `~/work/moquiwork/3p_other/`. Design line numbers are `design-purchasing.md:N`.
Mantle paths are relative to `runtime/component/mr-mantle/`. "D" = design-purchasing.md, "X" =
design-crosscut.md.

Pre-check disposition: the ASSERTED names at D:239 (`mantle.request.RequestServices`, a service file,
not an entity), D:794 (`InventoryReportServices.get#PriceHistoryInfo` exists), D:989 (the design correctly
says these are commented out), D:678 (`hold#Invoice`/`release#InvoiceHold` follow "Proposed") and
D:1148–1160 (needs-table entities) are dismissed. The rest of the names it lists are proposed in context.
The registry gap for §3.11 is partly dismissed: Projects §6 has a Purchasing row (design-projects.md:1286),
but the design's tabs differ from it (R-PUR-20). The registry gap for §3.15 is dismissed: Commons §4 covers it
(design-drawers-commons.md:536). The naming finding is confirmed (R-PUR-34), and so are the PUR-LATER needs
gap (R-PUR-27) and the Quotes.xml placeholder (R-PUR-40). The Vendor/* inventory blanks are not
Purchasing's forms, but the design must name them in Decided (R-PUR-33). FindOrder CreateSalesOrder is
Sales' form and is dismissed. The blank `create#PurchaseShipmentInvoices` service is Accounting's to fill
(design-accounting.md:1848) and is dismissed.
